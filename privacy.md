# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Personal Backup App is a private-use utility operated by the Google account owner.

## Google Drive access

The utility uses Google Drive access authorized by the owner to upload, download, verify and manage encrypted backup files and their checksums.

Backup contents are encrypted before upload. Google Drive stores the encrypted files and associated metadata.

## Use and sharing

Google user data is used only for the owner's backup and recovery operations. It is not sold, used for advertising or shared with third parties except Google as the storage provider.

## Retention and control

Backup files remain until deleted by the owner or the configured retention process. The owner can revoke the utility's access through their Google Account settings. Revoking access does not delete existing backups.
